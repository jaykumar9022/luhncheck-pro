How the Luhn algorithm works

1. Start from the right

The last digit is the check digit. It is never doubled.

2. Double every second digit

Begin with the digit next to the check digit and skip one each time.

3. Subtract 9 when over 9

A doubled 7 becomes 14, then 5. Same as adding its digits.

4. Add everything up

Sum the doubled and unchanged digits.

5. Check divisibility

A total divisible by 10 passes.

6. Know the limits

Luhn catches typos only. It does not prove a number is real or active.
